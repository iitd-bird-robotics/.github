# CoE-BIRD, IIT Delhi

Official GitHub organization for the Centre of Excellence on Bi-Inspired Robotics and Drones (CoE-BIRD), IIT Delhi.

---

The Centre of Excellence on Biologically Inspired Robots and Drones (CoE-BIRD) at IIT Delhi is an interdisciplinary research centre that aims to advance bio-inspired robotics for real-world autonomy.

We design intelligent robotic systems, from assistive wearables to field-deployed drones, that operate reliably in complex, unstructured environments. 
Our work spans human-centred robotics, soft and bio-inspired systems, and autonomous field robotics, all unified by advances in embodied intelligence, learning, and control.

## Research Areas

- Human-Centered and Assistive Robotics
- Soft and Bio-Inspired Robotics
- Autonomous Field Robotics
- Cross-Cutting: Embodied Intelligence Learning and Control
